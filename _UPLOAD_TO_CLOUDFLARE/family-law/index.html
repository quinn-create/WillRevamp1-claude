Putting your children first

Family cases are different from every other kind of case. The other side is someone you once planned a future with. The people with the most at stake are often too young to speak for themselves.

Will keeps the focus where it belongs: on your children, and on a parenting plan that gives them stability. Some issues can be worked out by cooperative negotiation; others need to be argued before the court. The consultation is where you start to see which path fits your case.

Case types

Family matters Will Fraley handles

  • Divorce

    Filing for divorce, and the issues it brings with it: child support, property division and spousal support.

    Divorce

  • Child custody

    Deciding who makes choices for your child, and whether custody is sole or shared, through a parenting plan built for stability.

    Child custody

  • Visitation

    Time with your child matters. A parent who is denied visitation can file a petition asking the court to enforce it.

    Visitation

  • Parenting plan modifications

    A move, a change in money, or parents who no longer agree can be reasons to revisit a plan. Will can help you propose a change or answer one.

    Parenting plan modifications

  • Paternity

    Establishing that a child is yours, or contesting a claim that is not true.

    Paternity

  • Adoption

    Stepparent, relative, private, agency and foster care adoptions. The filings and the court appearance are handled for you.

    Adoption

  • DCS cases

    Contacted by the Department of Children’s Services? Get help responding, preparing for interviews or court, and working toward reunification.

    DCS cases

When domestic violence is part of the picture

Will gives legal counsel when domestic violence comes up in a family case, and every allegation is taken seriously. If you are also facing a criminal charge, the same office handles domestic assault defense.

The process

How a family case moves

  1. 01

    The first conversation.

    You explain what is happening at home and what you want for your children. Call (615) 410-7290 for a free initial consultation.

  2. 02

    Naming the issues.

    Common disputes include custody and visitation, child support, property division and spousal support.

  3. 03

    Working toward agreement.

    Spouses on amicable terms may be able to settle some disputes between themselves, or with help from a mediator.

  4. 04

    Court, when needed.

    If agreement isn’t possible, Will argues your side before the court.

  5. 05

    Life after the order.

    Circumstances change. When they do, Will can help you propose a change to the parenting plan.

Questions

Questions about family law

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Call (615) 410-7290 for a free initial consultation.

Which counties do you cover for family cases?

Rutherford, Coffee and Wilson counties.

We already have a parenting plan. Can it be changed?

It can be revisited when circumstances change, such as a move or a change in either parent’s finances. Will can help you ask for a change, or respond if your former spouse wants one.

Do you handle child support?

Yes. Child support comes up in divorce and custody cases, and Will handles it in both.

Can I get help in Spanish?

Yes. Se habla español, and Spanish-speaking services are available.
