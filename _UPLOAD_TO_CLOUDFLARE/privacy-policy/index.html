Privacy Policy

This page explains what the website of Will Fraley, Attorney at Law collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: the site collects very little. It sells nothing about you. Analytics and marketing tools stay off unless the firm turns them on and you say yes.

Last reviewed: October 3, 2026

The short version

  • What we collect

    What you type into the contact form, if you choose to send it. Basic technical data the host needs to deliver the page.

  • What we don’t do

    We do not sell your personal information. We do not load analytics or marketing tools before you agree.

  • What you control

    Your choices live on Cookie Settings. Change them any time. A Global Privacy Control signal counts as a “no”.

Who we are

This website belongs to Will Fraley, Attorney at Law, 509 W College St, Murfreesboro, TN 37130.

In this policy, “we” and “the office” mean the firm. “The site” means willfraleylaw.com and its Spanish pages under /es/.

How the site is built

The site is a set of plain, pre-built pages. It has no logins, no accounts, no comments and no shopping cart.

It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you open a page, Cloudflare receives the technical details every web request carries: your IP address, your browser and device type, the page you asked for and the time. Cloudflare uses them to deliver the page and to protect the site from attacks. We do not use them to identify you.

The site’s fonts and images are served from the site itself, not from outside font or image services.

The contact form

The contact form is not switched on yet. Until it is, nothing you type on the contact page is sent anywhere. To reach the office, call (615) 410-7290 or email inbox@willfraleylaw.com.

Please don’t send confidential details

Do not put confidential information in the form or in a first email. Sending a message does not create an attorney-client relationship. Save the details for your consultation.

Analytics and marketing tools

The site is built so that analytics and marketing tools are off by default. The firm decides whether to turn any of them on. Even then, none of them loads until you say yes on the consent banner or on Cookie Settings.

Right now, no analytics or marketing tools are turned on. No tracking cookies are set, whatever you choose.

If the firm turns a tool on later, it will be listed below, and the site will ask for your consent again.

What these tools never receive

Marketing and analytics tools never receive what you type into the contact form. If you say no, or never answer, they do not load at all.

How your choice is stored

Your answer is saved on your own device in two places: a first-party cookie named consent and a matching entry in your browser’s local storage.

They hold four things: your answer for analytics, your answer for marketing, the version of this policy you answered, and the date and time you chose. Nothing about your choice is sent to a server.

If this policy changes in a way that affects your choices, the version number changes and the site asks you again. Clearing your browser data also clears your choice.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, the site treats it as a “no” to analytics and marketing. Those tools do not load while the signal is on, and the switches on Cookie Settings show as off.

We don’t sell your information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not trade it or rent it to anyone.

We share it only with the services named on this page, and only so they can do the job described here. We may also disclose it when the law requires it.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, such as Google Maps for directions and the firm’s Facebook page. Those sites have their own privacy policies. This policy stops at our door.

Children

This site is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them through it. If you believe a child has sent us information through the site, contact the office and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Messages sent through the site are kept in the office inbox as long as the office needs them to respond to you. If you become a client, your file is kept as a lawyer’s professional duties require.

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent through the site. Email inbox@willfraleylaw.com or call (615) 410-7290. If the law or a lawyer’s duties require us to keep something, we will tell you.

Security

Every page is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of sending information over the internet is perfectly secure, which is one more reason to keep confidential details for your consultation.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the “Last reviewed” date at the top changes with it. If the change affects what the site collects, the site asks for your consent again.
