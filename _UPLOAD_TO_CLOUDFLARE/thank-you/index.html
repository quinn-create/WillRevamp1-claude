  • While you wait

    • Keep any papers you were given together in one place: court notices, citations, bond papers, letters.
    • Write down dates, names and what happened while it’s fresh.
    • Keep your phone nearby, and check that your voicemail isn’t full.
    • Don’t send confidential details by email. Save them for your consultation.
