About Will Fraley

Why people call Will Fraley

Criminal court and family court are his focus. Will dedicates his practice to defending clients in criminal court and advocating for parents and spouses in family court. If a charge and a family case land at the same time, you can talk to one lawyer about both.

Your case gets personal attention. Will Fraley and his team personally handle each case, no matter how big or small.

He is from here. Will is a Tennessee native who moved to Murfreesboro in 1992 to attend Middle Tennessee State University. He made the city his home and built his private practice here.

He keeps training. Will has completed advanced cross-examination training with the Tennessee Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers.

He is part of the local bar. Will is a member of the Tennessee Bar Association, the Rutherford and Cannon County Bar Association and the Tennessee Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers.

He serves the whole area. Will represents clients in Murfreesboro and the surrounding counties of Rutherford, Coffee and Wilson.
