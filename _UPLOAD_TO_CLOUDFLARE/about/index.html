About Will Fraley

Will Fraley grew up around the law: his parents both practiced law in Fayetteville, Tennessee.

Today Will Fraley, Attorney at Law, defends clients in criminal court and advocates for parents and spouses in family court. He has practiced law since 2004.

Will’s story

His full name is Raymond Wilford Fraley, III, but he is better known as Will.

Will moved to Murfreesboro in 1992 to attend Middle Tennessee State University. At MTSU he was a member of the baseball team. To this day, Will shares this philosophy:

“Baseball is the greatest game, and discipline is the key to life.”

A Tennessee native, Will came to love Murfreesboro. He made it his home, and the place where he would build his private law practice.

From law school to his own practice

Will earned his law degree from Nashville School of Law in 2004. While in law school, he worked as a court officer for Judge Ben Hall McFarlin, Jr.

He began practicing law in 2004. Later he opened his own full-service private practice, handling criminal and family law matters.

How Will practices

Criminal court and family court. Will’s practice is built around criminal court and family court. He also handles personal injury cases.

Your case stays personal. Will Fraley and his team personally handle each case, no matter how big or small.

A trial attorney. Will is a dedicated trial attorney. When a case has to be tried, that matters.

Local reach. He represents clients in Murfreesboro and the surrounding counties of Rutherford, Coffee and Wilson.

Training Will has completed

Will has completed two training programs with the Tennessee Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (TACDL), including advanced cross-examination training. Both certificates of completion are shown below.

  • Advanced Cross-Examination Training

    Will completed the 2019 TACDL Advanced Cross Examination Training at Montgomery Bell Park in Burns, Tennessee. The certificate of completion is dated September 20, 2019.

  • Tennessee Criminal Defense College

    Will completed the 5th Annual Tennessee Criminal Defense College, held March 30 to April 1, 2006, at Montgomery Bell State Park. The certificate is issued to R. Wilford Fraley, III.

Katie Fults, Attorney at Law

Katie Fults is an attorney who guides clients through some of life’s most challenging transitions with compassion, integrity and a steady hand.

Her practice focuses on family law, including divorce, custody matters and adoption.

Before becoming an attorney, Katie spent a decade working with one of Murfreesboro’s law firms. She earned her undergraduate degree in Organizational Communication from Middle Tennessee State University in 2014. She earned her law degree in 2022 and has practiced law ever since.

Katie is an active member of the Tennessee Bar Association, the Rutherford and Cannon County Bar Association, and the Middle Tennessee Lawyers Association for Women. She represents clients across Middle Tennessee, including Rutherford, Coffee, Bedford, Cannon and Grundy counties, among others.
