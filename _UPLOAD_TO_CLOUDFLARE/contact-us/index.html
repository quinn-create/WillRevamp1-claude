What happens after you reach out

  1. 01

    You call or write.

    Give your name, a good phone number and a line about what happened. You don’t need to explain everything yet.

  2. 02

    We set up a consultation.

    The office schedules an initial consultation with you.

  3. 03

    You talk through your options.

    The consultation is where you discuss your situation and your legal options.

  4. 04

    You decide.

    You choose whether to go forward. Nothing is decided for you on that first call.
