Small charge or serious one, it deserves a careful look

Some drug charges start with a traffic stop and a small amount of marijuana. Others start with a search warrant and a word like “distribution.” Both can turn your life upside down.

Either way, the charge is the state’s version of events. Your side has not been heard yet. Will Fraley and his team personally handle criminal cases, no matter how big or small.

Drug charges Will Fraley handles

Will Fraley defends people charged with possession and with distribution. That includes the charges below and any other drug-related offense.

  • Possession or use of a dangerous drug

    Charged with having or using a controlled drug, whether it was found on you, in a car or in a home.

  • Marijuana possession

    Possession of marijuana charges, including ones that began with a routine stop.

  • Drug paraphernalia

    Charges for pipes, scales, baggies or other items police say were used with drugs.

  • Manufacture of a dangerous drug

    Accused of making a controlled drug, or of helping to make one.

  • Distribution

    Charges that say drugs were sold or meant for sale, not just for personal use.

  • Drugs and driving

    A driver may be considered under the influence of controlled substances, including marijuana. See DUI defense.

What a careful defense asks

Every drug case turns on its own facts. These are the kinds of questions worth working through with your lawyer.

  • How did police find the drugs? A stop, a search of a car, a search of a home, or something you were asked to hand over.
  • Who else had access? A shared car, a shared room or a borrowed bag can matter.
  • What is the substance, really? What the reports say, and what the lab says.
  • What was said, and when? What you told officers, and what they told you.

You don’t need answers to all of these before you call. Bring what you know, and the papers you were given.

If your son, daughter or spouse was charged

A family member can make the first call, even one who has never dealt with a criminal case before. You don’t need to know the legal name of the charge.

Have your family member’s full name ready, along with what you know about the arrest and any court date. Then call (615) 410-7290 for a free consultation. Spanish-speaking services are available.

A few things to avoid

  • Don’t explain it away to police or friends. Save the full story for your lawyer.
  • Don’t post about it. Photos, messages and comments can end up in a case file.
  • Don’t miss a court date. If you are unsure of the date, the papers you were given should say. Bring them to your consultation.

Questions

Drug charge questions

It’s a small possession charge. Is it worth calling a lawyer?

Yes. Will Fraley and his team personally handle criminal cases, no matter how big or small. The consultation is free. Call (615) 410-7290.

Do you handle distribution charges, or only possession?

Both. Will provides representation for people charged with drug possession and drug distribution.

The drugs weren’t mine. What should I do?

Don’t argue the point with police or post about it. Write down who else used the car, room or bag, and bring that list to your consultation.

I was charged after a traffic stop. Is this a DUI case or a drug case?

It can be both. A driver may be considered under the influence of controlled substances, including marijuana. Will handles DUI and drug charges.
