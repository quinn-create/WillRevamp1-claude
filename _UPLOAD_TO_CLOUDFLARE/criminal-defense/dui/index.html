The night is over. The case is just starting.

A DUI arrest happens in minutes. The case that follows takes longer, and that gives you time to prepare.

You’ve been charged, not convicted. Before your first court date, you should know what the charge means, what could happen and what your choices are. That is what the free consultation at (615) 410-7290 is for.

What you may be facing

Your first question may be about your driver’s license. Penalties for DUI can include fines, license suspension, probation, mandatory DUI classes and jail.

What a person faces depends on the facts of the case. Possible jail time, for example, can vary with the blood alcohol level and with any prior DUI on the driver’s record.

A DUI is not only about alcohol: a driver may be considered under the influence of other intoxicants and controlled substances, including marijuana.

Penalties change, and every record is different. At your consultation, you and Will discuss your legal options for your charge, in plain terms.

What to do this week

  • Keep every paper. The citation, bond papers, and any notice about your license or your court date.
  • Write down the stop. Where and when it happened, what the officer asked, and which tests you took or declined.
  • Keep it offline. Talk about the case with your lawyer, not on social media or in group texts.
  • Call before your court date. Call (615) 410-7290 for a free consultation.

    The stop and the charge.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    The review.

Questions

DUI questions

Do you handle a first-offense DUI?

Yes. Will Fraley handles first and second offense DUI, felony DUI and underage DUI.

Can I be charged with DUI if I wasn’t drinking?

Yes. A driver may be considered under the influence of other intoxicants and controlled substances, including marijuana. If drugs were found in the car, see drug crime defense too.

My teenager was charged. Can you help?

Yes. Will handles DUI cases for adults and for minors. Tennessee has a zero-tolerance policy for underage drinking and driving, so take the charge seriously from day one.

Will my DUI case go to trial?

Not every case does. Some end in an agreement and others go to trial; if yours does, you have a dedicated trial attorney beside you.
