## Putting your children first

Family cases are different from every other kind of case. The other side is someone you once planned a future with. The people with the most at stake are often too young to speak for themselves.

Will keeps the focus where it belongs: on your children, and on a parenting plan that gives them stability. {fact:F210} Some issues can be worked out by cooperative negotiation; others need to be argued before the court. {fact:F313} The consultation is where you start to see which path fits your case.

## How a family case moves

1. **The first conversation.** You explain what is happening at home and what you want for your children. Call [(615) 410-7290](tel:+16154107290) for a free initial consultation. {fact:F013,F096}
2. **Naming the issues.** Common disputes include custody and visitation, child support, property division and spousal support. {fact:F204,F201,F202,F203}
3. **Working toward agreement.** Spouses on amicable terms may be able to settle some disputes between themselves, or with help from a mediator. {fact:F266}
4. **Court, when needed.** If agreement isn't possible, Will argues your side before the court. {fact:F313}
5. **Life after the order.** Circumstances change. When they do, Will can help you propose a change to the parenting plan. {fact:F223,F221}

Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
