## What to gather before your consultation

You don't need a perfect file. Bring what you can find:

- **Any court papers** you have filed or been handed.
- **A rough list** of what you own and what you owe, together and separately.
- **Recent pay stubs or tax returns**, yours and your spouse's if you have them.
- **Your children's routine**: school, activities, who usually handles what.
- **Your questions**, written down, so nothing gets lost in the moment.
