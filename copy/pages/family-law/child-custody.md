## How a court looks at custody

Custody decisions focus on the best interests of the child, so the question is what arrangement serves your child, not which parent wins an argument. {fact:F290,F270}

That is why it helps to build your side of a custody case on the everyday facts of your child's life rather than on the conflict between the adults.

## What to write down now

Start a simple record while details are fresh. It helps at your consultation and later if the case goes to court.

- **The daily routine.** Who handles mornings, school drop-off, meals, homework and bedtime.
- **Time with each parent.** A plain calendar of who had the children, and when.
- **Messages with the other parent.** Save them. Keep your own short, calm and about the children.
- **School and medical contacts.** Teachers, doctors and caregivers who know your child.
- **Real safety concerns.** What happened and when, written down plainly.

Before you move out, move the children or change their routine, talk to a lawyer first.
