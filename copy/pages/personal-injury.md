## What a claim can cover

An injury reaches past the day it happened. A personal injury claim can seek damages for past and future medical bills, lost income, emotional distress, and pain and suffering. {fact:F245}

What your claim may be worth depends on the facts of your case, so the conversation starts with those facts, not with a number.
