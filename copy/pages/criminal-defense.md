## How a criminal case moves

1. **The charge.** A case starts with an arrest or a citation and a first court date. Bring every paper you received to your consultation.
2. **The consultation.** You tell your side. You learn, in plain terms, what the charge means and what your options are.
3. **The review.** Your lawyer studies the charge, the reports and the evidence, and looks for weak points in the case against you.
4. **The options.** Will looks for every lawful way to get the charges reduced or dismissed. {fact:F150} Some cases are resolved by agreement; others go to trial.
5. **Court.** If your case goes to trial, you have a trial attorney beside you. {fact:F056}

Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
