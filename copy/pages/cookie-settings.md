## The three categories

### Strictly necessary · Always on
These keep the site working and remember your answer on this page. They can't be switched off, and they do not track you.

- **Your consent choice.** A first-party cookie named `consent` and a matching entry in your browser's local storage. They store your answers, the policy version and the date and time you chose.
- **Security.** Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may use a security cookie to protect it from attacks and bots.
- **Spam check.** If the contact form is on and uses Cloudflare Turnstile, the check runs only on pages that show the form.

### Analytics · Off unless you say yes
These show the firm which pages help people and which don't, so the site can be improved. They never receive what you type into the contact form.

[vendor: ga4]
- **Google Analytics 4.** Counts visits, pages read and how people arrived. Sets cookies such as `_ga`.

[vendor: clarity]
- **Microsoft Clarity.** Shows clicks, scrolling and where readers stop, with form typing hidden. Sets cookies such as `_clck` and `_clsk`.

[vendor-off: analytics]
No analytics tools are turned on at the moment.

### Marketing · Off unless you say yes
These let the firm measure its ads and show ads to people who have visited the site. They never receive what you type into the contact form.

[vendor: meta]
- **Meta Pixel.** Tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) about your visit. Sets cookies such as `_fbp`.

[vendor: tiktok]
- **TikTok Pixel.** Tells TikTok about your visit. Sets cookies such as `_ttp`.

[vendor-off: marketing]
No marketing tools are turned on at the moment.

## If you change your mind

Turning a category off stops it from loading on your next page. Cookies it already set may stay in your browser until they expire. You can delete them in your browser's settings.

Clearing your browser data also clears your saved choice. The banner will ask again on your next visit.
