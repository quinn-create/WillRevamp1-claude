## What to gather first

Bring what you have. Will can tell you what else your adoption needs.

- The child's birth certificate.
- Any custody, guardianship or other court orders involving the child.
- Your marriage certificate, if you are a stepparent.
- Agency or placement paperwork, for an agency or foster care adoption.
- Names and contact details you have for the child's birth parents.
