## What a careful defense asks

Every drug case turns on its own facts. These are the kinds of questions worth working through with your lawyer.

- **How did police find the drugs?** A stop, a search of a car, a search of a home, or something you were asked to hand over.
- **Who else had access?** A shared car, a shared room or a borrowed bag can matter.
- **What is the substance, really?** What the reports say, and what the lab says.
- **What was said, and when?** What you told officers, and what they told you.

You don't need answers to all of these before you call. Bring what you know, and the papers you were given.

- **Don't explain it away to police or friends.** Save the full story for your lawyer.
