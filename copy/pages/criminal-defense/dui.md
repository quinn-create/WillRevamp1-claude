## What you may be facing

Your first question may be about your driver's license. Penalties for DUI can include fines, license suspension, probation, mandatory DUI classes and jail. {fact:F281}

What a person faces depends on the facts of the case. Possible jail time, for example, can vary with the blood alcohol level and with any prior DUI on the driver's record. {fact:F304}

A DUI is not only about alcohol: a driver may be considered under the influence of other intoxicants and controlled substances, including marijuana. {fact:F305}

Penalties change, and every record is different. At your consultation, you and Will discuss your legal options for your charge, in plain terms. {fact:F090,F099}
