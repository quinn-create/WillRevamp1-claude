## What not to do

Most of what protects you this week is restraint.

- **Don't contact the person who accused you.** Not by text, not through a friend, not to "clear the air."
- **Don't post, and don't delete.** Stay off social media about the case. Leave messages, photos and accounts as they are.
- **Don't agree to "just talk."** If a detective or investigator asks you to come in, call a lawyer before you go.
- **Don't tell more people than you must.** Until you have talked to a lawyer, keep the circle small.
