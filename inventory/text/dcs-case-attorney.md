### Protecting Your Parental Rights

A DCS case can be one of the most stressful and emotional experiences a parent can face. Allegations of neglect or abuse can lead to investigations, hearings, and even temporary removal of children from the home. Our firm provides strong legal representation to help you respond appropriately to DCS inquiries, prepare for interviews or court appearances, and work toward reunification whenever possible.We’ll help you understand your rights, the DCS process, and the steps you can take to demonstrate that your home is safe, stable, and in your child’s best interest.

### How We Can Help in DCS Cases

Our Murfreesboro DCS attorney handles a wide range of matters, including:

DCS investigations and interviews

Protective custody and removal hearings

Dependency and neglect cases

Termination of parental rights (TPR)

Reunification and permanency plans

Foster care placement and court reviews

We understand the seriousness of these situations and will stand beside you to help ensure that due process is followed and your parental rights are not violated.
