## Division of property

In divorce cases in Tennessee, a judge using a principle called “equitable distribution” handles the division of property. What this means is that the judge can weigh various factors to divide things in a manner that he or she deems to be fair. Although fault is not a consideration, other things are, such as the length of time the marriage has lasted, who contributed what financially, and the needs that both spouses have. We can assist you in making sure you present your case clearly.
