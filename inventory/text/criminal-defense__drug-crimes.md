## Drug Crimes

## Have you been convicted of drug possession?

If you have been charged with a drug-related crime, such as the manufacture of a dangerous drug, possession or use of a dangerous drug, possession of marijuana, possession of drug paraphernalia or any other drug-related offense, you need an experienced defense attorney. Any criminal conviction can cause serious and sometimes irreparable harm. Aside from the possibility of fines, probation or incarceration, a criminal conviction can profoundly damage your reputation, your career and other parts of your life. If you or someone you love has been accused of a crime, it is crucial to have an experienced criminal defense attorney on your side. Our experience has shown us that there is no substitute for diligent preparation and investigation.
