### First-Degree Criminal Sexual Conduct in Tennessee

Tennessee state law defines certain sex crimes as “first-degree” criminal sexual conduct. If a person is found guilty of committing first-degree criminal sexual conduct, they will likely face serious penalties, such as time in prison.

A sex crime is classified as first-degree sexual conduct in the state of Tennessee if:

- The victim was underage

- The perpetrator used physical force and/or a weapon in the crime

- The victim was mentally or physically incapacitated

Every case is different, and it’s possible that other factors in a case can lead to charges being categorized as first-degree criminal sexual conduct. We can provide further information on how your charges may be classified, potential consequences, and the defense strategies we can use.
