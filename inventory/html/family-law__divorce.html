Divorce Law Services in Murfreesboro

Turn to the team at Will Fraley Attorney at Law for assistance with a family law case such as divorce in the Murfreesboro area. Our firm has the experience to handle all the details for you so that this difficult process can be navigated as quickly and simply as possible. We understand this is a very trying time, and we’ll do everything in our power to assist you with the legal aspect of the proceedings so that it can be resolved and you can move on with your life.

Divorce is never easy. Many complex and confusing issues arise during the process. An attorney will work closely with you, listening to your concerns, educating you in your options, and understanding your goals. We think that personalized service is something that is especially important in legal matters, and we’ll work hard to make sure we are representing you in the way you need.

Child support

If you have children with your partner, the issue of child support will likely need to be addressed. This can not only be a delicate matter to broach, but it is often one that is extremely difficult to resolve. There are many factors to consider when trying to establish child support amounts, and we can help you walk through all the ones that may pertain to your given situation, so you have a better knowledge of your rights and responsibilities. If there is negotiating to be done, we can handle that on your behalf as well, so you don’t have to deal directly with your partner on the issue.

Child custody and parenting time

Custody of minor children can be a contentious issue in divorce cases. Emotions run high, and it can be a great help to have someone handling all the details, so you don’t have to tend to them. We work with our clients to present a neat, well-organized bid for custody that takes all the legal factors into account. We’re on your side, and we’ll work hard for you.

Spousal maintenance

If you are in a situation where you will be filing for spousal maintenance, we can help you get the job done. We’ve worked with many clients facing divorce to fight for a fair and equitable agreement between you and your spouse. Although this can be extremely challenging, we’re up to the task. When you want to make sure your rights are represented well, contact our firm.

Division of property

In divorce cases in Tennessee, a judge using a principle called “equitable distribution” handles the division of property. What this means is that the judge can weigh various factors to divide things in a manner that he or she deems to be fair. Although fault is not a consideration, other things are, such as the length of time the marriage has lasted, who contributed what financially, and the needs that both spouses have. We can assist you in making sure you present your case clearly.

Paternity

As a family law attorney, we understand the various factors involved in paternity suits. If you find yourself in need of this type of service during a divorce, we can help advise you and take care of any paperwork that needs to be filed to prove parenthood.

Adoption

Adoption situations are always a little complicated, and if you have adopted a child during your marriage, custody will still need to be decided. We can review the adoption paperwork carefully and talk with you about what to file in court in these unique situations.

Contact us for a divorce consultation.
