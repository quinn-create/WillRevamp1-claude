# Direction C "Neighbor": generated-image slots (set `C`)

For A15 (image director). There are exactly three slots. Use model `nano_banana_pro` at `resolution: "2k"`.
All three are scenes only. **No people, no faces, no hands, no silhouettes, no figures seen in windows or
reflections, no pets, no text, no signage, no lettering, no numbers, no house numbers, no license plates, no
clock faces, no flags with readable marks.** Never show gavels, scales, handcuffs or any crime imagery.
None of these images shows, or implies, the firm's real office at 509 W College St. They are generic Middle
Tennessee places, never captioned as the office. Each one is decorative (`alt=""`). Grade all three as one set.

**Set grade (all three):** warm, low golden light, or the soft warm light just after rain. The look is
natural and photographic, not illustrated. Choose the palette words below to sit on the cream page.

- **Highlights:** warm cream and pale honey. Never pure white.
- **Mid-tones:** sand, warm stone and aged red brick.
- **Shadows:** soft warm umber that fades toward a deep blue-grey. Never black, never muddy brown.
- **Blue:** keep it small. Use dusk sky or a cool shadow note that echoes the logo blue.
- **Brick:** brick-red is the main warm color, at moderate saturation.

Use gentle contrast. There is no lens flare, no heavy vignette, no HDR halo and no tilt. Match the warmth of
Will's real desk photo (IMG03: honey wood, cream wall, soft window light). The hero sits beside that photo
inset, so the two must read as the same world.

Every prompt ends with: "no people, no faces, no text, no signage, no lettering".

## 1. `hero` (16:9, Home, full-bleed photographic hero; the one full-bleed treatment on the page)

- **Subject:** a historic Middle Tennessee courthouse-square townscape at golden hour. It shows a row of
  two-story red-brick storefronts with ornate painted cornices and tall upper windows. Mature shade trees
  line a quiet, empty brick-edged sidewalk. A wrought-iron bench and a planter stand in the foreground. In
  the soft background stands a white courthouse cupola that is generic, not a recognizable landmark.
  - The storefront glass is plain or reflects warm light only.
  - There are no awnings with words, no window lettering, no banners and no parked vehicles.
- **Mood words:** rooted, warm, neighborly, golden, familiar, home, unhurried.
- **Palette (words):**
  - aged red brick and warm terracotta on the facades;
  - cream and ivory painted cornices and window trim;
  - honey-gold sunlight on the upper stories;
  - deep green, just-turning foliage;
  - soft warm-umber shade at street level;
  - a pale, clear sky moving from warm cream at the horizon to a soft muted blue above.
- **Light / time:** golden hour, about 40 minutes before sunset. Low sun rakes from the right, so the
  cornices glow and the sidewalk falls into soft shade. Long, gentle shadows. A clear evening.
- **Lens / composition:**
  - 35 mm equivalent, at eye level from across the street, looking slightly down the block.
  - Verticals are kept straight.
  - The storefront row and the cupola sit in the right 55% of the frame.
  - The left 45% is calm: soft tree shade, sky and out-of-focus foliage. Keep it free of detail. The inset
    card with the H1, the phone number and Will's photo sits there on desktop.
  - The image must survive a 4:5 crop on phones, centered at about 62% of the width, with the cupola and one
    cornice still in frame.
  - Medium depth of field: the background stays recognizable but soft.
- **Mood / palette cue from the old site:** IMG08 (`mainstage-v1-img.webp`, the old Home hero). Take its red
  brick with crisp white trim, its tall civic tower and its mature autumn trees. Re-light them at golden hour
  and set them in an older, smaller courthouse-square scale. Do not reproduce that building or its
  glass-and-brick modern wing.
