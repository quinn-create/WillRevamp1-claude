# Direction C — "Neighbor"

**The idea in one sentence:** Will Fraley is Murfreesboro's own lawyer, so the site should feel like his town in late-afternoon light, with his real photo and phone number waiting in a calm card.

## Why it fits this firm

The people who call are scared: someone charged last night, a parent in a custody fight, a family calling for the first time in Spanish (REVAMP-BRIEF §2). The brand audit (A07) found three things worth keeping: the logo blue, two honest photos of Will, and a Murfreesboro rootedness that the old stock photos and screenshots buried. The overall audit scored the old look 4/10. "Neighbor" keeps what is true and puts a real-feeling place behind it. Will is a Tennessee native who came to Murfreesboro for MTSU and made it home. Warm, local design says that before anyone reads a word.

## Palette

- **Cream** #F6F1EA: page ground. **Card** #FCFAF6. **Sand** #E9DFD1: alternate bands.
- **Warm ink** #2B2420: text and footer. **Muted** #6B5E55.
- **Logo blue** #447CB7: wordmark and large type. **Link blue** #2F5F95: links and the call button. **Navy** #1D478A: the deep band.
- **Brick** #96452F: the one accent, for eyebrows, step numbers, focus rings and the secondary button.

Every text pair passes WCAG AA.

## Type

**Fraunces** (display) and **Figtree** (text), both free Google Fonts and self-hosted. Fraunces is a warm old-style serif. Its soft and quirky settings stay off, so headings read as serious, not quaint. Figtree is a clear, friendly sans that holds up on a phone at night, with full Spanish accents.

## The logo

The blue wordmark sits on the cream header without a box, at full strength. Blue is the only cool color on the page, so the logo, the links and the call button read as one family.

## Best at

Approachability. A worried parent or a first-time Spanish-speaking caller feels they have reached a local person. The photographic hero gives Home a sense of place that the other directions lack.

## Risks

Warmth can slide into folksy. Serious copy, a restrained serif and no clip art guard against it. A generated townscape could be mistaken for the real office, so it is never captioned that way. The photo hero is the heaviest page to load and needs a strict image budget.
