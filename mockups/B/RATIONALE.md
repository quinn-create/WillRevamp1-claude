# Direction B: "Verdict"

**The idea in one sentence:** the person charged last night should know what to do, with the call button
under their thumb, before they finish the first screen.

## Why it fits

Most visitors are on a phone, scared, often late at night or the morning after an arrest (copy/VOICE.md).
The audit found the current site makes calling hard. Outside the FAQ the number never appears in body text,
there are about ten button wordings, and pages take about eight seconds to load on a phone (audit/AUDIT.md).
Verdict answers with structure, not volume: bold color blocks mark where each part of the page starts. One
gold button means "call now", and it is the only gold on the page. The brand audit (audit/A07.md) scored the
old look 4/10 but named real equity: the logo blue, the navy bands and the persistent phone bar. Verdict
keeps all three.

## Palette

- **Night** #0B1220: hero, footer, body ink
- **Navy** #1D478A: brand bands, links, focus ring on light grounds
- **Logo blue** #447CB7: large type, rules, fills only
- **Sky tint** #EAF1F9: alternate bands
- **Signal gold** #F2B544: call and submit buttons, mobile call bar, never decoration
- **Slate** #4B5768: secondary text
- **White** #FFFFFF: ground

All pairings pass AA (declared in `tokens.css`). If gold reads as cheap, the fallback is brick #8A3F2A with
white text.

## Type

**Archivo** for display, heavy and slightly expanded through its width axis, with **Inter** for text.
Archivo holds its shape at 72 px without shouting; Inter stays readable at 17–18 px on a cheap phone. Both
cover Spanish accents and are self-hosted variable files, so nothing loads from Google at runtime.

## The logo

The blue wordmark sits on the white glass header, as drawn. On night blocks and in the
footer it reverses to a white knockout of the same letterforms. It never sits on gold or on the logo blue.

## Best at, and what it risks

**Best at:** conversion. The call button is in the first screen at every width and repeats mid-page and in
the footer.

**Risks:** heavy blocks can feel loud or cold to a parent in a custody dispute, and gold tips toward
billboard if it spreads. So gold stays on the call action only, the copy stays steady and plain, and nothing
trades on fear or promises an outcome.
