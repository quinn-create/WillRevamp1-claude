# Direction B "Verdict": generated-image slots (set `B`)

For A15 (image director). Exactly three slots. Model `nano_banana_pro`, `resolution: "2k"`. All three are
scenes only. **No people, no faces, no hands, no silhouettes, no reflections of figures, no text, no signage,
no lettering, no numbers, no flags with readable marks.** No gavels, scales, handcuffs or crime-scene imagery.
None of these images shows or implies the firm's real office at 509 W College St. Each is decorative
(`alt=""`) and is graded as one set. In the Verdict layout the images act as color blocks: the CSS lays a navy
duotone over them where type sits, so each frame needs one large, calm, low-detail area.

**Set grade (all three):** hard, clean light with crisp shadow edges; deep shadows that fall toward navy and
near-black, never brown or muddy; highlights in limestone white and pale sky blue; mid-tones in cool slate
grey. Warm notes stay small, like warm wood or one lamp pool. Never a large gold area, because gold on the page
is reserved for the call button. Saturation is moderate, contrast is high, and there is no haze, no lens
flare and no vignette.

Every prompt ends with: "no people, no faces, no text, no signage, no lettering".

---

## 1. `hero` (16:9, Home, full-bleed night hero, the one full-bleed treatment on the page)

- **Subject:** a bold low-angle view up a row of classical courthouse columns and the cornice and entablature
  above them, against a clear, deep-blue sky. Plain limestone or painted-white columns, with brick in the
  far edge of frame. The building is generic Southern civic architecture, not a recognizable landmark. Any
  frieze is left blank, with no carved words.
- **Mood words:** decisive, upright, strong, high-contrast, clear, steady.
- **Palette (words):** deep navy sky grading to near-black at the top edge; limestone white on the sunlit
  column faces; cool slate-grey and navy shadow in the flutes; one small note of warm red brick.
- **Light / time:** hard late-afternoon sun from the right, about 30 degrees above the horizon, raking across
  the flutes so every column throws a crisp vertical shadow. Cloudless.
- **Lens / composition:** 24 mm equivalent, low angle, verticals kept straight (tilt-shift corrected). Columns
  fill the right 45% of the frame. The left 55% is open sky and deep shadow, kept clean for the H1 and the
  gold call button. Strong vertical rhythm. Horizon not visible. Must survive a center-right crop to 4:5 on
  phones, so keep the strongest column at about 65% of the width.
- **Mood / palette cue from the old site:** IMG08 (`mainstage-v1-img.webp`, the civic building on the old Home
  hero): its saturated deep-blue sky, crisp white trim against red brick, and hard clear-day sun. Take only the
  light and the sky. Do not reproduce that building.

## 2. `practice` (3:2, Criminal Defense hub, color-block panel beside the H1 on a navy band)

- **Subject:** steep, wide stone courthouse steps rising toward a pair of closed, heavy paneled wooden doors
  at the top. Steps fill the lower two-thirds of the frame. Plain stone cheek walls, with no railings carrying
  signs, no plaques and no door numbers.
- **Mood words:** resolve, forward motion, clarity, weight, order.
- **Palette (words):** pale limestone and cool grey stone; step shadows in deep navy-slate; the doors in dark
  walnut, almost near-black; a sliver of pale sky-blue at the top edge, or none.
- **Light / time:** mid-morning hard side light raking from the left, so each tread casts a sharp graphic band
  of shadow and the steps read as a stripe pattern. Clear day.
- **Lens / composition:** 35 mm equivalent, from the foot of the steps, slightly left of center and looking up
  about 15 degrees. Minimal and geometric, with strong diagonals leading the eye up to the doors in the upper
  third. Keep the left third quieter so it tolerates a navy duotone. No foliage clutter.
- **Mood / palette cue from the old site:** IMG08 (the civic building's portico, entry steps and white stone
  in hard sun) for the stone, the light and the civic scale; IMG09 (the brick office's dark wooden double
  doors) for the door tone only. This must not resemble the 509 W College St entrance: no brick arch and no
  house number.

## 3. `contact` (4:5, Contact page, panel beside the form on a night block)

- **Subject:** a quiet law-office desk at dusk. A classic dark desk telephone with its handset resting in the
  cradle, the keypad turned away from camera or soft out of focus so no digits read. Beside it, a closed,
  plain dark-blue case folder with a blank cover and no label or tab text, and a closed pen on top.
  Dark wood desktop. A single focused desk lamp casts one warm pool of light across the phone and folder.
  Behind, out of focus, a tall window showing deep-blue dusk sky.
- **Mood words:** ready, direct, now, focused light, calm, private.
- **Palette (words):** near-black and deep navy shadows filling most of the frame; dark walnut wood; the window
  in deep twilight blue; one small, contained warm lamp pool, warm white rather than gold; the folder in muted
  navy.
- **Light / time:** blue hour, just after sunset. The only key light is the desk lamp from upper left, with
  falloff to near-black within a short distance. Faint cool fill from the window.
- **Lens / composition:** 50 mm equivalent, about 30 degrees above desk height, shallow depth of field (f/2.8
  look). Phone and folder sit in the lower-middle third. The top 35% is dark window and wall, kept calm for
  the H1 overlay on phones. Must survive a 1:1 center crop. No clutter, no screens, no books with spines, no
  framed certificates and no diplomas.
- **Mood / palette cue from the old site:** IMG09 (`Screenshot-2025-10-21-at-2.27.41-PM.png`, the brick office
  exterior used as the old Contact hero) for its dark wood tones and its clear, cool blue sky, here carried
  indoors as the dusk window. A generic interior only. It is never captioned or implied to be the real office.
