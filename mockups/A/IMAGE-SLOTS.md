# Direction A "Counsel": generated image slots (set `A`)

For A15 (Image Director). There are exactly three slots. Each one is a **scene only**: no people, faces,
hands, silhouettes, reflections of people, text, signage, lettering, flags with emblems or license plates.
No generated building is captioned or implied to be the real office at 509 W College St or any real
courthouse. All three are decorative (`alt=""`).

**One grade for the whole set:** overcast Middle Tennessee light, cool blue-grey shadow, low-saturation
brick, warm paper-white highlights, and blacks that sit near blue-black ink, never pure black. The images
should read like the opening plate of a book, not like advertising.

---
