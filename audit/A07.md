# A07: Visual brand audit (old site)

Score: 4/10

Equity: the logo blue and two honest attorney photos. The rest is stock, Mac screenshots and Elementor defaults in five typefaces.

## Evidence

- **Logo (IMG01, `Logo-3.webp`).**
  - Format: 400×62 raster WebP with a transparent background. No vector, no favicon, no mark.
  - Design: "WILL" in a hairline sans, "FRALEY" in a medium sans, and "ATTORNEY AT LAW" tracked wide between hairline rules.
  - Colors (sampled with sharp, 1,916 opaque px): wordmark **#447CB7**, one flat blue. Tagline and rules are **#113964** at about 40% alpha, which renders near #A0B0C1 on white (2.2:1).
  - At its header width of about 140 px, the tagline cannot be read.
- **Palette** (kit `post-6.css`, confirmed by sampling screenshot pixels):
  - Logo blue **#457CB7**: buttons, call bar, Contact tab. White on it is 4.36:1, so large text only.
  - Navy **#1D478A**: bands. White on it is 9.05:1.
  - Sky **#6EC1E4**: subheads, active sidebar item. 2.02:1 on white, which fails.
  - Slate #6E7783 and grays #F5F7F7 / #ECF0F1.
  - Footer text: #324A6D and #1C244B.
  - Inline overrides: #00CCFF and #3366FF.
  - Gravity Forms defaults on 23 pages: #204CE5, #001AB3, #527EFF.
  - Untouched kit defaults: #61CE70, #7A7A7A.
- **Type.**
  - Five families load at 18 variants each: Roboto, Roboto Slab, Lato, Poppins, Raleway.
  - Four are visible on Home: Raleway headings, Lato body, Poppins call bar and footer, Roboto icon titles.
  - `post-57` and `post-10` declare 11 sizes (14, 16, 17, 18, 22, 23, 27, 28, 34, 45, 65 px), so there is no scale.
  - Body text is justified on Home and About, which leaves rivers at 390 px.
- **Spacing.**
  - The container is 1,140 px with percentage padding.
  - The Home contact block leaves about 360 px empty on its left.
  - About has about 200 px of dead space between bios.
  - The fixed "Call Today" bar covers text in the 1280 captures of /about/, /family-law/divorce/, /legal-services/ and /contact-us/. On /about/ the pull quote is clipped.
  - On mobile, the practice tiles are icon-only.
- **Imagery.**
  - Four Mac screenshots serve as heroes and OG images (IMG09–IMG12, 0.99–3.61 MB PNGs).
  - IMG04 is the attorney mirrored inside a baked-in frame.
  - IMG14–IMG29 are 16 stock cards: handcuffs, crime tape, cash. More stock: the gavel hero, and the scales in the "Why" band.
  - Every photo sits on a light-blue-to-white gradient offset block.
  - The best images are authentic but low-res: IMG03 (Will at his desk, 910 px) and IMG02 (brick doorway, 673 px). IMG05 (Katie, 169×224) works only as an avatar.
- **Consistency.** Two template families:
  - Hubs: split hero with a stock photo.
  - Detail pages: a navy band, no imagery, a 21-link sidebar, and an H1 that repeats the band.

## Brand equity that must survive

1. **The wordmark and #447CB7.** The theme's #457CB7 already comes from the logo.
2. **The attorney's photography.** IMG03 and IMG02, plus Katie's headshot (IMG05). Never replace them with generated images.
3. **Murfreesboro rootedness.** Keep:
   - the "Murfreesboro Lawyer" H1;
   - "Native to Tennessee" and "Se habla español";
   - the red-brick, courthouse-square feel, regenerated and never captioned as the office.
4. **The persistent phone band.** "Call Today (615) 410-7290".

## KEEP
- The wordmark, brand blue #447CB7 and navy #1D478A as the deep ground.
- IMG02, IMG03, IMG05, plus the certificates IMG06 and IMG07. Crop, color-correct and convert to AVIF only.
- The phone band, made non-overlapping.
- Brick warmth as the secondary accent.

## FIX
- **Logo.** Redraw as SVG, with a solid tagline at 4.5:1 or better (or drop the tagline below 200 px wide). Add a favicon and a mark.
- **Text-size blue.** Use about #356BA6 (5.5:1) for links and small type. Keep #447CB7 for large type and fills.
- **Sky subheads.** Change them to navy.
- **Type system.** One serif display face plus one sans, on a 1.25 scale, self-hosted and subset.
- **Text setting.** Left-aligned, 60–75 characters per line, never justified.
- **Detail pages.** Give them a real hero and grouped related links instead of the sidebar.
- **Mobile tiles.** Label them.
- **IMG04.** If it is used at all, flip it back and crop off the frame.

## KILL
- The Mac-screenshot heroes and OG images (IMG09–IMG12).
- All stock photos (IMG14–IMG29, gavel, handcuffs, scales).
- Gradient offset blocks and gradient overlays.
- Inline #00CCFF and #3366FF, Gravity Forms blues and default kit colors.
- Raleway, Poppins, Roboto and Roboto Slab.
- Half-empty columns, and the fixed bar that covers text.

## Top 5 moves
1. **Rebuild the logo as SVG and derive tokens from it.** Brand #447CB7, text-blue #356BA6, navy #1D478A, a brick accent, paper and ink. Contrast-check them in one tokens file and allow no inline colors.
2. **Put the attorney's photos at the center.** Use IMG03 and IMG02 in framed or split layouts, color-matched, with written alt text. Generated Murfreesboro scenes fill the other hero slots.
3. **Set one type system.** Serif display plus humanist sans, a 1.25 scale and a 68-character measure. Leave room for Spanish strings about 20% longer.
4. **Use one template family.** Shared hero, 8 px spacing grid, cards and phone band. The sidebar and the gradient frames go.
5. **Replace the 21 stock and screenshot slots with logged Higgsfield scenes.** One grade throughout: overcast Middle Tennessee light, warm brick, cool blue shadow. No people, no faces, no text.
