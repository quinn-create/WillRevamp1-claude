# A09 — Trust and compliance audit (old site)

Score: 3/10

The footer disclaimer is sound and nothing says "specialist" or "certified". The rest falls short: there is no privacy policy, accessibility statement, consent mechanism or results disclaimer. The SMS line points to a policy that doesn't exist, and "expert" appears 4 times.

## Evidence

**Privacy and consent**
- 0 of 24 pages mention "privacy", "cookie" or "accessibility" (inventory/text, inventory/html).
- GA4 `G-MN6QWEVWM4` (gtag/googletagmanager) loads on every page with no banner and no opt-out.
- 23 Gravity Forms (inventory/forms.json) collect name, email, phone and a 600-character message, with no confidentiality warning or privacy notice.

**SMS/TCPA line**
- 21 forms: "By submitting, you agree to be contacted about your request & other information using automated technology. … Text STOP to cancel. Acceptable Use Policy".
- "Acceptable Use Policy" is plain text in a `<p>` with no link (html/about.html), and no such page exists.
- Consent is bundled into submitting, with no checkbox.

**Disclaimer**
- Footer on all pages: "…for general information purposes only. Nothing on this site should be taken as legal advice… receipt or viewing does not constitute, an attorney-client relationship."

**Expert and comparative language** (RPC 7.1; law-firm.md rules 1 and 3)
- /contact-us/: "Our criminal defense attorney and family attorney is an expert in addressing felonies and misdemeanours…"
- /legal-services/: "the experts at Will Fraley, Attorney at Law will develop a unique legal strategy"
- /legal-services/: "Our attorneys will take on cases others may not feel confident in winning." (unsubstantiated comparison)
- /criminal-defense/dui/: "ready to put his expertise and knowledge to work"
- /personal-injury/: "we obtain the best possible recovery for our clients"
- "aggressive(ly)": 6 times, 4 pages (warn list).
- "specialist", "specialize", "certified", "guarantee": 0 hits.

**Results disclaimer**
- No page carries one. The testimonials ("achieve a fair settlement", "dedicated to making sure I would win") repeat on 21 pages. /contact-us/: "reduce or dismiss the charges".

**Testimonials (C15)**
- Katherine S. stops at "…I highly recommend" (F133).
- Eddie W.: "Mr. Fraley is the man you." (F134).
- 36 star elements on testimonials.html, with no review platform named (F139).

**Credentials (C08, C19)**
- 21 pages list "Tennessee Trial Lawyers Association". /about/ lists "Tennessee Association for Justice" and "Criminal Defenses Lawyers" instead.
- TACDL appears only as course certificates (2006, 2019). Bar admission is never stated.
- "Our attorneys" and "the experts" suggest a team, but Katie Fults appears only on /about/ (C05).

**Spanish (C13)**
- "full bilingual Spanish services": 21 pages.
- "Spanish Speaking Services Available": sidebar.
- "Se habla español.": /about/ and meta descriptions.
- Who provides the service is never stated.

**Free consultation (C14)**
- 35 mentions across 24 pages, worded 4 different ways.
- The phone number is missing from the sentence on 23 pages. "Call or contact us online for a free consultation" (21 pages) has an empty slot where the phone should be. /about/: "Schedule a free consultation online or at to get started with Law Offices of Will Fraley." /family-law/visitation/: "…online or at to discuss your legal concerns in a free initial consultation".

**Accessibility**
- No statement. audit/axe-before.json: 73 serious violations across 24 pages. Color-contrast and a missing h1 fail on every page.

## KEEP
- The disclaimer's substance, in the footer of every EN and ES page.
- The 3 testimonials, verbatim with their published attribution. Typos and truncation stay.
- "Se habla español" and "full bilingual Spanish services", exactly as ledgered (F092, F093; rule 10), never expanded.
- The free consultation (F090), always beside `tel:+16154107290`.
- The certificate scans, shown as completed training.

## FIX
- Memberships: use only the 3 shared groups (C08) and correct "Defenses".
- Results disclaimer (EN and ES) on every page with a testimonial or an outcome.
- Replace "expert" with "focuses on" or "handles". Say "the firm" instead of "our attorneys" until C05 is answered.
- Form: a non-confidentiality notice above submit, a link to Privacy, and SMS consent as an optional unchecked box, OFF by default.
- One consultation sentence, with the phone number always present.

## KILL
- The dead "Acceptable Use Policy" text and the "& other information using automated technology" catch-all.
- "…online or at to get started", "…online or at to discuss" and the phoneless "Call or contact us online".
- "best possible recovery", "cases others may not feel confident in winning", "reduce or dismiss the charges" and "aggressive" in every form.
- Unsourced star graphics and any aggregateRating.
- GA4 loading before consent.

## Top 5 moves
1. **Ship Privacy, Cookie Settings and Accessibility Statement pages (EN and ES)** and link them in every footer. Tracking ships OFF and needs consent.
2. **Rebuild the form's legal text:** a non-confidentiality notice, a Privacy link, and optional unchecked SMS consent that stays disabled until the owner confirms texting. Drop the "Acceptable Use Policy" text.
3. **Remove "expert" and all comparative or outcome claims sitewide.** Put the results disclaimer on every page with a testimonial. QA greps for these words.
4. **Show testimonials verbatim with no stars.** Ask the owner for the full Katherine S. text, the platform each review came from, and client consent.
5. **Hold credentials and Spanish claims to the ledger:** the 3 shared memberships, training rather than certification, and the Spanish lines verbatim. Ask the owner to verify bar standing on tbpr.org, current memberships and who provides Spanish service.
