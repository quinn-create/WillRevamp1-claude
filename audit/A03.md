# A03 — Content & Messaging Audit (old willfraleylaw.com)

Score: 4/10

## Evidence

- **Offer clarity.** None of the 24 pages has an H1 (`pages.json` h1Count = 0). The experience claim appears in 11 versions (18 / almost 20 / 20+ / over 20, CONFLICTS C01). The home page puts "18 years" under a "20+ Years" heading (`text/index.md` L20–22). "Murfreeesboro" is misspelled in 19 titles (C02).
- **Duplicated copy.** I measured 8-word shingles across `inventory/text/*.md`.
  - **Boilerplate:** It makes up 34–96% of each page: the "Why Will Fraley" block (21 pages), badges, SMS text and the testimonial carousel. The Katherine S. review appears 44 times.
  - **Stub pages:** Theft and Drug Crimes are 91% and 93% shared, with only about 125–150 unique words each. 57% and 69% of the rest is copied from the `/criminal-defense/` hub.
  - **Siblings:** After removing boilerplate, the criminal sub-pages overlap about 0% with each other, and so do the family sub-pages. The hubs share 38% (criminal) and 1% (family) with their children.
  - **Conclusion:** The problem is the copied block plus two stubs, not siblings copying each other.
- **Audience.** On criminal pages the first testimonial is about a divorce (`theft-1280.png`). Drug Crimes asks "Have you been *convicted*…" when the reader has been charged. No page speaks to families of someone arrested. Spanish appears on 21 pages only as the English line "full bilingual Spanish services" or "Se habla español". The site has no Spanish page, no hreflang and no Spanish form.
- **Proof.** There are 3 testimonials: one cut off, one missing a word, all with unsourced stars (C15). There are no case results. Memberships differ between Home and About (C08), and the certificates exist only as images (C19). "In the News" holds 1 real article (C16).
- **CTA logic.** The phone number appears in body copy on 1 of 24 pages (`faqs.md` L30). Elsewhere the copy says "Call or contact us online" without it. The broken "…online or at to get started" sentence appears on the home, about and visitation pages. The form has 7 fields, 5 required, plus "Text STOP to cancel" SMS consent text (`forms.json`). At 1280 px the sticky call bar covers body text. The consultation is worded 5 ways (C14).
- **Reading level.** Flesch-Kincaid grade is about 9.3 (median), ranging from 8.2 on Theft to 11.2 on Contact (sentences average 22.8 words there). That is too high for stressed readers on phones.
- **Dead ends.** Adoption and DCS have no meta description. FAQs has no form. The "Wills" card links back to its own page (C09). "Latest News & Blog" points to "#", and each page has 9–11 hash-only links. The criminal hub grid leaves out Sex Crimes and adds a "Murder" card (C10). There are 2 broken 404s. Personal Injury is a single page with no next step.
- **RPC 7.1 risk.** The copy says "is an expert" (Contact), "premier… lawyers" and "our attorneys" (Legal Services), and "aggressive" (Theft). The DUI and theft figures may be stale or contradictory (C17, C18).
