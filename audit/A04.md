# A04 — SEO & local search audit (willfraleylaw.com)

Score: 4/10

The URL tree and NAP are solid, but on-page template errors hit all 24 pages.

## Evidence

- **H1:** none on any page (`h1Count` 0 ×24, `inventory/pages.json`). The first heading on every page is the sidebar's h3 "Contact Us".
- **Titles:** the "Murfreeesboro" typo appears in 15 of 24 titles, and in the site name, `og:site_name` and Organization name on all 24. 9 titles run over 60 characters (/faqs/ is 70).
- **Descriptions:** missing on /adoption/ and /dcs-case-attorney/. Three are over 160 characters (/ 174, parenting-plan 164, paternity 162). The sex-crimes description starts "ere you accused" and the personal-injury one has "Injuried". 10 pages use the boilerplate "X. Our legal team… Call us today!".
- **JSON-LD:** the markup sets up two unlinked entities. One is the Yoast Organization, named "…in Murfreeesboro". The other is a hand-added LegalService named "Attorneys at Law", in the plural (C02). The LegalService block has these errors:
  - It says Friday closes at 17:00, but the footer says 4:00 pm (C07).
  - Its `areaServed` adds Davidson and Williamson counties, which the page copy never mentions (C06).

  Elsewhere, /faqs/ carries 3 `FAQPage` blocks, and a `SearchAction` points to `?s=`.
- **Alt text:** there are 102 images. All 72 logo instances share one alt. Every one of the other 30 images, all content images, has an empty alt. That includes the portrait, the certificates and 13 service cards.
- **Links:**
  - 10 links point to the staging host `beige-baboon-435532.hostingersite.com` (on /legal-services/, /criminal-defense/dui/ and /criminal-defense/domestic-assault/).
  - 2 `www.` links return 404.
  - 12 hub cards link through a 301.
  - "In The News" and "Blog" link to `#`.
  - Every page carries a designer credit link.
- **Thin/duplicate:** /contact-us/ has 223 words and /testimonials/ 373. The "Why" block and the sidebar repeat on 21 pages. Custody, divorce and visitation pages overlap on custody and visitation topics.
- **Indexability:** /page/2/ is a 200 duplicate of the home page. /category/uncategorized/ is an empty archive with a self canonical. Both feeds are empty but linked in every `<head>`. The site has no `hreflang` and no Spanish URLs, despite "Se habla español".
- **Local:**
  - NAP is consistent: the phone appears as "(615) 410-7290" 76×, there is one address, and `geo` is set.
  - The map link is a deprecated `goo.gl/maps` short link.
  - No page targets a service area.
  - Testimonials show 5-star graphics from no named platform (C15). There is no review markup, and that is correct.

## KEEP

- All 24 paths, including the `/criminal-defense/*` and `/family-law/*` hierarchy.
- Self canonicals and `index, follow`.
- The NAP exactly as written, and the LegalService + `geo` + `sameAs` concept.
- The single-intent practice pages (700–1,374 words).
- The existing legacy 301s.

## FIX

- One H1 per page naming the service and Murfreesboro. Sidebar and footer headings stop being headings.
- Titles of 60 characters or less: "{Service} Lawyer in Murfreesboro, TN | Will Fraley". Remove the typo everywhere.
- A unique 140–155 character description on every page.
- One linked `@graph` with:
  - LegalService/Attorney "Will Fraley, Attorney at Law"
  - Friday closing at `16:00`
  - `areaServed` set to Murfreesboro, Rutherford, Coffee and Wilson
  - a Person and a BreadcrumbList
  - one FAQPage, on /faqs/ only
- Descriptive alt on content images. Generated scenes get `alt=""`.
- Direct final-URL links only.
- `hreflang` en/es/x-default on every pair.
- A full Google Maps place URL in place of the goo.gl link.

## KILL

- /page/2/, /category/uncategorized/, the feeds, the `wp-json`/oEmbed head links, the SearchAction and the designer credit.
- The Davidson and Williamson `areaServed` entries.
- The extra FAQPage blocks and the plural schema name.
- The star graphics. Never add `AggregateRating` or `Review` markup.
- The "Murder" hub card (C10). /criminal-defense/violent-crimes/ covers it.

## Top 5 moves

1. **Head template rebuild:** a unique H1, title and description on every EN/ES page.
2. **One schema graph:** correct name, hours and areaServed, plus Person, breadcrumbs and a single FAQPage.
3. **Full redirect map** (below) in `_redirects`, plus a Pages Function for `/?p=`. All 89 legacy URLs keep their equity.
4. **Link hygiene:**
   - Rewrite the staging, www, 404, 301-hop and `#` links.
   - Add contextual links between custody, visitation, parenting plans and paternity.
5. **Local + bilingual:** a `/es/` mirror with hreflang, one county list, and a GBP name, category and URL that match the site.
