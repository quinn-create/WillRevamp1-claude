# A08 — UX and conversion audit (old site)

Score: 4/10

The phone number is always one tap away on mobile. Everything else adds friction: the menu ships 4 times per page, there are about 10 CTA wordings, and a 7-field form sits about 60% of the way down each page. 13 CTA links are dead or point to a staging host.

## Evidence

**Nav depth** (inventory/html/*.html)
- The same 27-link menu renders 4 times per page (desktop, sticky clone, 2 dropdowns): 108 nav links per page.
- 15 practice pages add an 18-link "Legal Services" sidebar, for 174–177 links per page. index has 147.
- 3 levels deep (Practice Areas > Criminal Defense > DUI), 17 items under one parent.
- "In The News" and "Latest News & Blog" are `href="#"`.

**One primary action**
- 1280 header: "Contact Us" twice, 3 social icons, no phone (index-1280.png).
- CTA wording varies: "Contact Us Today" (20), "contact us online" (22), "Free Consultation" (5), "Schedule a free consultation online" (4) and more.
- Home hero jumps to /contact-us/ instead of its own form.
- Practice heroes have no CTA (criminal-defense__dui-390.png).

**Dead and stray CTAs**
- `href="#"`: "Contact Us Today" on /criminal-defense/dui/, /legal-services/ and /personal-injury/, and "Free Consultation" on /legal-services/.
- 9 links go to `beige-baboon-435532.hostingersite.com`: 3 on DUI, 5 on legal-services and 1 on domestic-assault.
- The broken "online or at" sentence appears on /about/ and /family-law/visitation/.

**Click-to-call**
- There are 3 `tel:` links per page. The footer link is `tel:+16154107290`. The 2 "Call Today (615) 410-7290" bars use `tel:16154107290`, without the `+`.
- Sticky: header `top`, call bar `bottom`. At 390 the bar is about 62 px, full width and in the thumb zone, but says nothing about what a call gets you.

**Form friction** (inventory/forms.json, `gform_1` on 23 pages)
- 7 inputs, 6 required: First, Last, Email, Phone, "When is the Best Time to Reach You?" (required, 6 options) and "Are you a New Client?" (3 options), plus a message capped at 600 characters.
- Placeholders act as labels.
- No confidentiality note, no "what happens next" line, and red-on-dark Submit text.
- The form sits at 56–67% of the HTML. On DUI at 390 it starts around y≈9,000 of 11,236 px.

**Mobile, 390 px**
- The fixed call bar covers the message field on /contact-us/ (contact-us-390.png) and body text on DUI.
- Home practice tiles lose their text labels at 390 (icons only).
- Footer links are about 13 px text on about 18 px spacing and the social icons are about 16 px. Both are below the 24 px target (WCAG 2.5.8).

**Trust near CTAs**
- The testimonial carousel sits just above the bottom form. Good placement, poor format (autoplay).
- The "Native to Tennessee / Trial Attorney / Spanish Speaking / Over 20 Years" tiles sit mid-page, away from every CTA. "Over 20 Years" conflicts with other year claims (CONFLICTS C01).
- "Se habla español" (F092) never appears beside the phone number. Hours (F031/F032) appear only in the footer and conflict with the schema (F033).

## KEEP
- The persistent mobile call bar with the full number as its label.
- Free consultation as the offer (F090, F030), always next to the `tel:` link.
- Testimonials placed directly before the closing CTA.
- The form above the fold on /contact-us/.

## FIX
- **Nav:** render once, 2 levels deep: Criminal Defense, Family Law, Personal Injury, About, Contact. Mobile uses an accordion. The header shows the phone link and one "Free consultation" button.
- **One primary action:** call first. The secondary action is a short form on the same page, reached by an in-page anchor. Use one CTA wording site-wide.
- **Form:** Name, Phone, Email (optional) and "Briefly, what happened?" (optional). Visible labels, autocomplete, a no-confidential-details / no attorney–client relationship note, a "what happens next" line and a thank-you page.
- **Call bar:** `tel:+16154107290`, at least 48 px tall, with safe-area padding and `scroll-padding-bottom` so it never covers fields. Micro-label: "Free consultation · Se habla español".
- **Tap targets:** at least 44×44 px; card labels at every width.
- **Trust at each CTA:** "Practicing law since 2004" (C01 safest form), Spanish spoken, hours and one verbatim testimonial.

## KILL
- The duplicate menus and the 18-link practice sidebar.
- The `#` "In The News" and blog items. News moves to /in-the-news/.
- The "Best Time to Reach You?" and "New Client?" fields.
- Every `href="#"` CTA and all 9 staging-host links.
- Header social icons and the duplicate "Contact Us" tile.
- The broken "online or at" sentence (rule 7).

## Top 5 moves
1. **Single-render, 2-level nav** with a phone link and one CTA in a sticky header no taller than 64 px. This cuts nav links from 108 to about 25 per page.
2. **4-field consultation form** on every practice page, reached by in-page anchors. It removes 2 selects and 3 required fields, stays OFF until configured and has `tel:` beside Submit.
3. **Call-first mobile pattern:** a 48 px bottom bar with `tel:+16154107290` and a trust micro-label. It never hides fields or focus.
4. **Trust strip at every CTA**, using ledgered facts only: since 2004 (F087), Se habla español (F092), hours (F031/F032) and one testimonial.
5. **CTA hygiene gate:** the build fails on `href="#"`, staging-host URLs or a `tel:` without `+`, and on any page with no primary CTA in the first 390 px viewport.
