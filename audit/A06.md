# A06 — Accessibility audit (old site, WCAG 2.2 AA)

Score: 4/10

Lighthouse's accessibility average of 90.1 (range 87–99) flatters the site. It has no H1, no skip link, invisible focus on 63% of tab stops, positive tabindex in the site-wide form, and contrast failures on all 24 pages.

## Evidence

**axe-core (audit/axe-before.json, 24 pages):** 0 critical, 73 serious, 120 moderate and 1 minor rule hits (494 nodes).

| Rule | Impact | Pages | Nodes | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tabindex (> 0) | serious | 23 | 184 | Contact form `#input_1_*`, 8 per page (all except FAQs) |
| color-contrast | serious | 24 | 133 | Light-blue headings, active nav item, submit button, inline links; legal-services has 25 |
| aria-prohibited-attr | serious | 22 | 106 | `aria-label="Read More"` on carousel icons |
| link-name | serious | 2 | 13 | Image-only links with `alt=""` (/criminal-defense/, /family-law/) |
| link-in-text-block | serious | 2 | 2 | Color-only links |
| landmark-unique | moderate | 24 | 40 | 4 unnamed `<nav>`s per page |
| landmark-no-duplicate-banner, landmark-banner-is-top-level | moderate | 24 | 48 | 3 `<header>`s incl. sticky spacer clone |
| page-has-heading-one | moderate | 24 | 24 | **No H1 on any page** |
| heading-order | moderate | 24 | 24 | Pages open on H3; footer jumps to H6 |
| empty-heading | minor | 1 | 1 | /about/ `h3:nth-child(4)` |

Lighthouse lows: 87 on /criminal-defense/, /family-law/ and /criminal-defense/domestic-assault/. High: 99 on /faqs/.

**Keyboard probe (axe-before.json `keyboard`):** 787 of 1,241 tab stops (63%) show no focus indicator (about 39 per practice page). `skipLink: false` on all 24 pages. `<main>` and `lang="en-US"` are present.

**Manual review (inventory/html, shots/before):**
- **Contrast:** index-1280.png shows light-blue hero subhead and H3s on white, a tiny gray footer legal line on blue, white copy over a photo, and blue form labels on dark blue.
- **Focus:** No custom `:focus` styles; Elementor defaults hide focus on menus, cards and the carousel.
- **Forms:** Labels are bound with `for` and "(Required)" is shown, but the name labels are screen-reader-only, so placeholders act as visible labels. Email and phone have no `autocomplete` token.
- **Link text:** "For Sitemap Click Here." is in the footer on all 24 pages, and 110 "Read More" icon labels.
- **Motion:** No `prefers-reduced-motion` rule exists. The testimonial carousel autoplays every 5 s on 22 pages with no visible pause control (2.2.2). There are 48 scroll-linked opacity effects.
- **Sticky UI:** A fixed header and a fixed call bar (`tel:16154107290`, no `+`) at 390 px risk failing focus-not-obscured (2.4.11).
- **Keyboard traps:** None found; the menu toggle has `aria-expanded` and the carousel arrows are labelled.
- **Text:** Body copy is justified (criminal-defense__dui-390.png), which creates rivers of white space.

## KEEP
- FAQs built on native `<details>`/`<summary>` (24 on /faqs/; Lighthouse 99).
- `lang` attribute and a single `<main>` per page.
- Programmatic labels and visible "(Required)" text on the form.
- `tel:` links on the phone number (72 across the site) and a persistent call path.
- Descriptive alt text on the logo ("Logo for Murfreesboro Attorney, Will Fraley").

## FIX
- One H1 per page that matches the page intent, then an H2/H3 outline with no skips. Footer headings become H2s or plain text.
- A visible `:focus-visible` ring with at least 3:1 contrast and a 2 px outline on every interactive element.
- Palette tokens checked to 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI. Replace the light-blue headings and the submit button colors.
- Form labels always visible, placeholders not used as labels, no positive tabindex, `autocomplete` tokens `given-name`, `family-name`, `email` and `tel`, and an error summary plus `aria-describedby` on errors.
- Image links get a text name: the card title inside the link, decorative image `alt=""`.
- Underline links inside body text.
- Name the landmarks: one `<header>`, `<nav aria-label="Primary">`, `<nav aria-label="Footer">`, `<footer>`.
- Sticky call bar: 44 px tall, never covers focused content, `href="tel:+16154107290"`.
- Left-aligned body text, lines of 70 characters or fewer.

## KILL
- Positive `tabindex` on the form fields (184 nodes).
- `aria-label="Read More"` on decorative icons.
- Testimonial carousel autoplay. Use a static list or a manual control with a visible pause.
- Scroll-linked opacity effects and parallax. Any motion is opt-in only, under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- The duplicate sticky-spacer `<header>`.
- "For Sitemap Click Here." Use a plain "Sitemap" link.

## Top 5 moves
1. **Semantic skeleton in the layout component:** a skip link to `#main`, one H1 per page, a sequential outline, and unique named landmarks. Clears 5 axe rules site-wide.
2. **Accessible design tokens before any mockup:** every text/background pair checked to 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI) and a site-wide `:focus-visible` ring. This fixes 133 contrast nodes and 787 invisible focus stops.
3. **Rebuilt consultation form:** visible labels, natural tab order, autocomplete, inline and summary errors, and the `tel:` link beside the CTA (rule 7). It stays off until configured.
4. **Motion policy:** no autoplay, no scroll effects, all motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and static testimonials quoted verbatim.
5. **Gate it:** axe plus a keyboard probe on every build (EN and `/es/`), with zero serious violations, a skip link present and visible focus on 100% of tab stops. Set `lang="es"` on the Spanish mirror.
