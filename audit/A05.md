# A05: Performance audit (old site, mobile Lighthouse)

Score: 3/10

The old site runs WordPress with Hello Biz, Elementor, Elementor Pro, Ultimate Addons and Gravity Forms. All 24 pages load the same heavy framework, so they are uniformly slow, though none is catastrophic.

## Evidence

**Source.** `audit/lighthouse-before.json`: 24 pages, mobile preset, 1 run each, loaded one at a time, 10 s apart.

**Caveat.** The sandbox proxy drops some requests (`failedRequests`: 2 on /criminal-defense/theft/ and /family-law/visitation/, 6 on /criminal-defense/sex-crimes/). Elementor also lazy-loads background images (20 `e-lazyload` containers on /criminal-defense/dui/). Weights are therefore a floor, so I report the median page.

**Thresholds.** LCP is over 2.5 s on 24 of 24 pages (over 4 s on 23). TBT is over 200 ms on 23. CLS is over 0.1 on 0. Four pages score under 50: /criminal-defense/ 45, /criminal-defense/domestic-assault/ 47, /legal-services/ 48, /family-law/ 49.

The hub image weight is full-size JPGs and PNG screenshots (`Screenshot-2025-10-21-at-12.58.05-PM-1536x1158.png`, `criminal-defense.jpg`). The best pages, /family-law/visitation/ (77, LCP 3.7 s) and /about/ (74, LCP 4.1 s), still miss 2.5 s.

**Framework overhead** (unique `.js`/`.css` URLs in `inventory/html/*.html`):
- **22 standard pages:** 56 assets each (27 JS, 29 CSS). 37 are Elementor (20 core, 11 Pro, 2 Ultimate Addons, 4 `uploads/elementor/css/post-*.css`), 9 jQuery, 8 Gravity Forms.
- **/contact-us/:** 48 assets, 29 Elementor.
- **/faqs/:** 39 assets, 30 Elementor, 5 jQuery, 0 Gravity Forms.
- Gravity Forms loads site-wide for one form (`gform_1`).

**HTML.** Each document is 126–162 KB before any asset loads (nested wrappers; 17 inline `data-settings` on the home page).

**Fonts.** Five Google families (Roboto, Roboto Slab, Lato, Poppins, Raleway), each requested at all 18 weight/italic variants, plus Font Awesome: about 219 KB per page. `display=swap` is set, which is why CLS stays low.

**Other code.** `wp-emoji`, Swiper (43 references), GA4 gtag (`G-MN6QWEVWM4`) and the Cloudflare challenge script `/cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/.../main.js`.

**Images.** The logo is already WebP. None of the home page's 5 `<img>` tags uses native `loading="lazy"`; Elementor lazy-loads with JS.

**Other scores (median).** Accessibility 90, best practices 81, SEO 100.

## KEEP
- Near-zero layout shift (CLS median 0.003). Match it with explicit image dimensions and `font-display: swap` plus metric-matched fallbacks.
- The WebP logo (IMG01), re-exported at 1x/2x as SVG or AVIF.
- SEO 100 on 22 of 24 pages; the new build must not regress.
- The URL structure (see A04); performance gives no reason to change it.

## FIX
- **Hub images** on /legal-services/, /family-law/, /criminal-defense/ (367–590 KB): AVIF/WebP with `srcset`, sized to the slot, 120 KB or less above the fold.
- **Certificate scans** (1536 px PNG screenshots): responsive WebP/AVIF, lazy-loaded, linking to the full-size file.
- **LCP:** inline critical CSS, preload the LCP image or hero font, `fetchpriority="high"` on the hero. Target 2.0 s or less on mobile.
- **Contact form:** ship it only where it renders, as native HTML with under 5 KB of JS, OFF until configured.
- **Analytics:** GA4 scaffolded off, loaded after consent and page idle, one tag.

## KILL
- Elementor, Elementor Pro, Ultimate Addons: 37 assets and about 430 KB of JS per page.
- jQuery and jQuery Migrate (9 assets).
- Gravity Forms on every page (8 assets).
- Swiper, Font Awesome, wp-emoji: replace with CSS scroll-snap or static layouts and inline SVG icons.
- 4 of the 5 font families and all 18-variant requests.
- The Cloudflare challenge script on content pages; static hosting needs no bot-challenge JS.

## Top 5 moves
1. **Static Astro output with zero framework JS by default.** Islands only for the mobile nav, accordion (or native `<details>`) and form. Budget: JS 30 KB or less per page (today 430 KB), TBT under 100 ms (today 369 ms).
2. **Font diet.** From 5 Google families × 18 variants to 1–2 self-hosted, subset `woff2` families (Latin + Latin-1 for Spanish). Preload the one heading weight. Budget: 100 KB or less, 4 files or fewer (today 219 KB).
3. **Responsive image pipeline** (Astro `<Image>`: AVIF/WebP, `srcset`, explicit dimensions, lazy below the fold, `fetchpriority` on the hero). Hub pages drop from 1.2–1.4 MB to under 400 KB.
4. **Critical CSS inlined plus one stylesheet of 30 KB or less** (today 160 KB in 29 files). This removes the render-blocking chain behind the 4.6 s FCP.
5. **Budgets enforced in QA.** Lighthouse on the built `dist/` for every EN and ES page (mobile, median of 3). Fail on LCP over 2.5 s, CLS over 0.05, TBT over 150 ms, weight over 500 KB or requests over 25.
