# Law-firm rules — Tennessee (binding for copy, build and QA)

Applies to Will Fraley, Attorney at Law, Murfreesboro, Tennessee. Source: Website Revamp Kit §7, ABA Model
Rule 7.1 and Tennessee Rules of Professional Conduct (Tenn. Sup. Ct. R. 8) RPC 7.1–7.5.

1. **No false or misleading statements** about the lawyer or the services (RPC 7.1). No comparison with
   other lawyers unless factually substantiated — so no "best", "top", "leading", "#1", "most
   experienced".
2. **Results.** Any mention of results, verdicts, outcomes, dismissals or settlements (including inside a
   testimonial) carries on the same page: "Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome."
   Spanish: "Los resultados anteriores no garantizan un resultado similar."
3. **No "specialist", "specializes", "expert" or "certified"** for the lawyer in any field unless the old
   site documents the certification and the certifying body. It does not (the two TACDL certificates are
   course-completion certificates, not specialty certification). Use "focuses on", "practices", "handles".
   ES: never "especialista", "experto", "certificado".
4. **People, names, bar numbers, admissions and memberships** come only from the facts ledger. Do not use
   "Esq." (the old site does not). Owner item (always present in `NEEDS-OWNER.md`): "Verify bar standing
   (tbpr.org) and that every listed association membership is current before launch."
5. **Footer legal line** on every page, EN and ES, keeping the substance of the old site's disclaimer:
   general information only; not legal advice; viewing the site or contacting the firm does not create an
   attorney-client relationship. EN must contain the words "not legal advice"; ES "no constituye
   asesoramiento legal".
6. **"Free consultation"** is allowed (the old site offers one) — always beside the phone number as a
   `tel:` link; never "guaranteed"; never time-limited unless the owner confirms.
7. **Testimonials:** only those already published, verbatim, with the published attribution. No edits, no
   composites, no AI-written reviews. Typos in the originals stay; the owner is asked for corrections.
8. **Contact form:** a short non-confidentiality notice above the submit button: do not send confidential
   information through the form; submitting it does not create an attorney-client relationship.
9. **Spanish pages make no claim the English pages do not make.**
10. **Spanish-language service:** the old site claims "Se habla español" and "full bilingual Spanish
    services". Keep exactly those claims (ledgered); do not expand them (e.g. do not say the attorney
    personally speaks Spanish).
11. **Advertising labels:** Tennessee does not require "Attorney Advertising" labels on firm websites; none
    is added. Recorded in DECISIONS for owner awareness.
12. **Second attorney.** Katie Fults appears only on the old About page. Keep her there as published; do not
    present the firm as multi-attorney elsewhere until the owner confirms.
